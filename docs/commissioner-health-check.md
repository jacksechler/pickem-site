# Commissioner health check

Open **Commissioner** and use **League diagnostics** to verify the active week before and during a weekend.

The check reports only operational counts and statuses. It never displays player pick answers. It verifies the eight-member roster, active week count, contender submissions and pick completeness, result-entry order, tiebreaker readiness, final score rows, connected live-score cache health, notification automation, and postseason configuration.

The postseason check expects the one-time seed bonuses **10 / 8 / 7 / 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 / 0**. The automated postseason database test simulates all four cuts from 8 to 6 to 4 to 2 to a champion.
